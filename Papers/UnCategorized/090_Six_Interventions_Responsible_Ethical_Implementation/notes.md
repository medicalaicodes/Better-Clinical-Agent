# Six Interventions for the Responsible and Ethical Implementation of Medical AI Agents
**Authors:** N/A
**Year:** 2026 | **Venue:** ArXiv
**ArXiv/DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.13743

# Problem Statement
The rapid deployment of autonomous medical agents outpaces current FDA regulatory frameworks, risking ethical breaches in patient care and equity.

# Key Contributions
1. Proposes a comprehensive ethical framework outlining six specific interventions for deploying medical agents safely.
2. Includes requirements for "Algorithmic Fidelity" (agents must not alter their behavior post-deployment without re-certification) and "Equity Auditing" (agents must be tested on minority demographics before activation).
3. Provides a checklist for hospital IT boards evaluating vendor agent solutions.

# Methodology / Architecture
A policy and ethics review paper. Synthesizes current FDA software-as-a-medical-device (SaMD) guidelines and adapts them for non-deterministic, multi-agent systems.
