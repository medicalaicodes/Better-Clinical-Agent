# Problem Statement
Current medical AI is often deployed as an "oracle" (a black box that spits out a diagnosis), leading to physician pushback, automation bias, and low clinical adoption.

# Key Contributions
1. Proposes a paradigm shift: Agents should not be diagnostic oracles, but "Collaborative Decision Mediators."
2. The agent's role is to present evidence, highlight conflicting data, and facilitate a dialogue with the human physician, acting as a peer rather than a replacement.
3. Introduces HCI (Human-Computer Interaction) guidelines for designing agentic UIs that foster appropriate reliance.

# Methodology / Architecture
A perspective/framework paper. It outlines a conceptual architecture where the Agent acts as a "Mediator." It retrieves patient data, visualizes the uncertainty (e.g., "70% chance of X, 30% chance of Y"), and prompts the physician with targeted questions to resolve the ambiguity.
