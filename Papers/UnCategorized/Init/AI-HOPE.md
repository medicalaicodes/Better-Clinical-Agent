# AI-HOPE: An AI-driven conversational agent for enhanced clinical and genomic data integration in precision medicine research

**Publication:** *Bioinformatics*, Volume 41 (7) (2025).
**Authors:** E.-W. Yang, E. Velazquez-Villarreal.

# Core Functionality and Purpose
*   **Natural Language Interaction**: AI-HOPE allows domain experts to perform complex bioinformatics analyses through natural language queries, removing the need for traditional programming expertise.
*   **Technical Workflow**: Powered by LLMs, it interprets user instructions, converts them into executable code (Python or R), and autonomously analyzes the data securely locally.
*   **Analytical Capabilities**: Supports statistical tasks such as association studies, clinical prevalence assessments, survival analyses (Kaplan-Meier curves, hazard ratios), and global variable scans.
*   **Security**: Closed-system design to prevent the leakage of sensitive clinical data.

# Research Impact and Ecosystem
*   Developed to bridge the gap between biomedical data and researchers, especially in oncology.
*   Has been expanded into a broader ecosystem of specialized pathway-centric agents:
    *   **AI-HOPE-TP53**: Focuses on TP53-driven molecular alterations.
    *   **AI-HOPE-TGFbeta**: Investigates TGF-β pathway dysregulation.
    *   **AI-HOPE-PI3K**: Analyzes PI3K pathway alterations.
    *   **AI-HOPE-WNT**: Designed to study WNT pathway dysregulation.
    *   **AI-HOPE-PM**: Focuses on population metrics and social determinants of health.
