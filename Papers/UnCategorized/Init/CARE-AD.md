# CARE-AD: Collaborative Analysis and Risk Evaluation for Alzheimer's Disease

**Publication:** *npj Digital Medicine* (August 2025).
**Authors:** Rumeng Li, Xun Wang, Dan Berlowitz, Jesse Mez, Honghuang Lin, and Hong Yu.
**DOI:** 10.1038/s41746-025-01940-4

# Key Details
*   **Objective**: The framework addresses the challenges of applying LLMs to complex clinical prediction tasks by using multiple autonomous "agents" to analyze longitudinal (over time) electronic health record (EHR) data.
*   **Architecture**: It deploys a collaborative suite of specialized LLM sub-agents. Each agent is assigned a specific domain role to extract signs and symptoms relevant to cognitive decline, simulating a multidisciplinary diagnostic board.
*   **Predictive Horizon**: Capable of predicting Alzheimer's disease risk up to 10 years prior to a patient's first official diagnosis code.
*   **Significance**: By leveraging multi-agent systems, CARE-AD aims to improve the accuracy and clinical interpretability of Alzheimer's disease risk assessment compared to traditional or single-model approaches.
